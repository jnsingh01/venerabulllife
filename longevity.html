Measurements and interventions

A measurement
Records something, such as how long you slept last night. On its own, it doesn’t show that anything will change, or that anyone will live longer.
An intervention
Something changed on purpose, such as a routine, a temperature or a supplement, and studied by comparing what happens under different conditions, with the outcomes measured. Some of the best-known longevity studies are in mice, and a finding in mice doesn’t show the same in people.
