How buying works

Choose here. Buy on eBay.

Every product here links to its own eBay listing, and you buy it there. For now, everything we sell is sold through our eBay store, Everyday Wellness.

Visit our eBay store (opens our eBay store in a new tab) Back to the shop

  1. Choose here

    Each product page shows what the package label says: the protein and servings, the size, the flavor and the date printed on the package, with photos of the package itself, front and back. If anything on a page ever differs from the label on the package, the label is right.

  2. Buy on eBay

    “Buy on eBay” opens the item’s eBay listing in a new tab. You pay on eBay, through the payment options eBay offers there. Prices on this site are the listed prices when they were last checked, and each page shows that date. eBay always shows the current price and whether the item is still available.

Questions

Is the price here the current price?

Each price here is the listed eBay price when it was last checked, shown with that date. eBay always shows the current price and whether the item is still available.

Where do I pay?

On eBay, through the payment options eBay offers there. There’s no checkout on this site.

Questions about an item

Use “Contact seller” on the item’s eBay listing, and the question reaches the seller through eBay.

About the products

Every item here is listed as New on eBay. Descriptions repeat what each package says about itself; this site doesn’t make health claims, and nothing here is medical advice. If you have an allergy or a health condition, read the full label in the photos, and ask your doctor if you’re unsure.

What’s on this site

Protein and nutrition is on this site today. Vitamins and supplements, skincare, and bath and body have their places here, with nothing listed in them yet.

Can I book the spa, or order from Rest?

Not yet. The Spa, Rest and Experiences are directions for Venerabull Life, not announcements. Nothing there can be booked or ordered yet.
